The last step feeds the first. That loop is what keeps it alive.

What gets automated is the writing, not the sending. The hours don’t go into pressing send — they go into “read the whole thread, find the receipt, work out their version, don’t misquote the price”.

The limits are a list. Five kinds of email may go out without a person, named one by one. Everything else waits for approval.
